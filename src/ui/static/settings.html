Memory

The memory stores information the system learns about you over time: your name, preferences, people, places, and other context.

Text-based store for requests, characteristics, people, places, relations, and other information about you.

Soul

The soul shapes the personality and tone of the assistant. It can be refined based on your feedback to better match how you want the assistant to communicate.

Text-based description of the assistant's personality, derived from your feedback to adapt its responses.
